THRIVE SYSTEMS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

Thrive Systems ("Thrive", "we", "us", or "our") provides business automation and artificial intelligence solutions designed to assist businesses with administrative, operational, communication, scheduling, accounting-related and other authorised workflows.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, processed, stored and protected when a user uses Thrive Systems or connects a third-party service to a Thrive Systems integration.

Information We Process

Depending on the services and integrations enabled by the user, Thrive Systems may process:

  • Business and contact information
  • Customer and client information
  • Appointment and scheduling information
  • Emails and business communications
  • Invoices, estimates, payments, products, services and other accounting information
  • Information obtained from third-party services that the user explicitly authorises Thrive Systems to access

We aim to access only information reasonably necessary to provide functionality requested or authorised by the user.

QuickBooks and Intuit Data

When a user connects a QuickBooks Online account to Thrive Systems, access is provided through Intuit's authorisation process.

Depending on the functionality authorised by the user, Thrive Systems may access or process QuickBooks information such as customers, products and services, invoices, payments, transactions, company information and related accounting records.

Thrive Systems does not obtain a user's QuickBooks password through the QuickBooks integration.

QuickBooks data is used to provide the automation or accounting-related functionality requested by the user. Thrive Systems does not sell QuickBooks data.

Users may revoke access to their QuickBooks integration when they no longer wish Thrive Systems to have authorised access.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide requested automation services
  • Retrieve and manage authorised business information
  • Create or update business records when authorised
  • Automate administrative workflows
  • Assist with scheduling and customer administration
  • Generate business summaries, reports or operational information
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure and improve the service
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Artificial Intelligence and Automated Processing

Certain Thrive Systems services may use artificial intelligence or automated systems to process information and perform tasks.

The scope of automated actions depends on the services configured for each user. Where appropriate, Thrive Systems may require user approval before financially consequential, destructive or sensitive actions are performed.

Users remain responsible for reviewing important business and financial records and ensuring automated actions are appropriate for their business.

Data Sharing

Thrive Systems does not sell personal information or customer business data.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide functionality requested by the user. These may include accounting platforms, communication providers, email services, scheduling platforms, cloud infrastructure providers and artificial intelligence service providers.

Third-party services operate according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Data Security

Thrive Systems takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. Users should maintain appropriate security practices for their own accounts and devices.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the applicable service, maintain required business records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or maintain system security.

Where technically and legally possible, users may request deletion of personal information held directly by Thrive Systems.

Third-Party Integrations

Thrive Systems may integrate with third-party platforms. Authorisation of a third-party integration does not transfer ownership of the user's data to Thrive Systems.

Users may revoke access through the relevant third-party platform where supported.

South African Privacy Law

Where applicable, personal information is handled in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

Individuals may request access to or correction of personal information held about them, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Thrive Systems develops or as legal, technical or operational requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the current version.
